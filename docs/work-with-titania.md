## The time-travelling camera {#camera}

<p><strong>Suitable for:</strong> weddings, company events, festivals, birthdays and public celebrations.</p>
<p>The time-travelling camera is a portable photographic experience somewhere between street theatre, performance and an old-fashioned photo studio.</p>
<p>Guests choose costumes and props, enter Titania’s improvised world and leave with a photograph from another possible century—or dimension.</p>
<p class="book-service__benefit">The experience invites guests to participate rather than merely watch—and gives them a personal photograph to take away.</p>
<p class="book-service__details"><strong>Space required:</strong> approximately 7 m²<br><strong>Minimum duration:</strong> two hours</p>

## Children and communities {#children}

<p><strong>Suitable for:</strong> kindergartens, schools, children’s festivals, family events and community organisations.</p>
<p>Titania works with children and families through clowning, games and social circus. Activities can include movement, improvisation, juggling and group games involving movement and dexterity—with room for different abilities and ways of participating.</p>
<p class="book-service__benefit">The activities can encourage empathy, communication, body awareness and self-confidence through play.</p>
