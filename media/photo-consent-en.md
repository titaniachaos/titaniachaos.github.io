# Photo consent — children's birthday party

**Draft.** Send with the booking confirmation, not on the day.

---

At the party I take photographs with my time-travelling camera. **Every child
takes their printed photo home** — that needs no permission; it is part of the
party.

Showing a picture **publicly** is a different thing: on my website, in a
portfolio, or to presenters. That needs your written agreement, and you can
withdraw it at any time.

You may agree to the party and not to publication. The party is unchanged
either way.
