# Photo and film release

**Working notes, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer read this before it is used
in earnest. What it tries to do is make the permission *specific enough to be
real* — the old version asked only about "the website", which does not cover
an Instagram post, and a release that does not name where a picture will go is
not informed consent.

Two forms follow: one for **adults** (workshop participants, colleagues,
performers, guests) and one for **children**, signed by a parent or guardian.
Use the right one. A child cannot consent for themselves.

---

## What is being asked, in plain words

Titania Chaos photographs and films her own work. Some of those pictures have
other people in them. Publishing a picture of somebody is a separate act from
taking it, and it needs their permission.

Three things make this permission strict rather than vague:

1. **It names the places.** Website, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, printed
   material, funding applications. Each is ticked separately. Not ticking one
   is a normal answer.
2. **It says what cannot be undone.** A picture posted to a social platform can
   be re-shared, screenshotted and copied by people who never saw this form. If
   permission is withdrawn later it will be removed from where it is controlled
   — the website, the accounts — but copies already made by others cannot be
   recalled. Anyone signing should understand that before they tick the social
   media boxes, not afterwards.
3. **It says the platforms are not neutral.** Instagram, Facebook and YouTube
   are run by companies outside the EU. Posting a picture there gives that
   company a broad licence under its own terms and transfers personal data out
   of the EU. That is a real consequence and it belongs on the form.

Permission is free to give, free to refuse and free to withdraw. Refusing
changes nothing about taking part in the workshop, the party or the show.

## For whoever administers this

**Controller:** Tatiana Petkova, Diehlgasse 6/10, 1050 Vienna, Austria ·
`agent@tatianapetkova.com`

**Legal basis:** consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); for a child, Art. 8 with the
guardian's agreement. In Austria the right to one's own likeness (§ 78 UrhG)
applies in addition to data protection, and it is not satisfied by a privacy
notice alone — it needs the person's agreement.

**Keep the signed form.** Consent that cannot be evidenced is consent you do
not have (Art. 7(1)). Scan it, file it against the date and the event, and
record which boxes were ticked — a form ticked for "website" only does not
license an Instagram post.

**When it is withdrawn**, remove the frame from `FRAMES` in
`docs/.vitepress/media.data.ts` and re-run `node media/make-media.mjs`. The
derived files go with it. Then remove it from any social post it was used in.

**What this does not cover:** anyone who is merely in the background of a
public place and is not the subject of the picture; and other people's
artworks, which need the artist's permission rather than a sitter's.
