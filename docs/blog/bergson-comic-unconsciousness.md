# Henri Bergson — comic unconsciousness

**Status:** Verified

**Source finding:** Bergson connects comic effect with a character's limited awareness of what others can see.

**For this project:** This can be compared with clown traditions in which noticing failure becomes part of the comic event. The two positions do not need to be reconciled in theory.

**Studio question:** When does awareness strengthen a comic moment, and when does it end it?

**Reference:** Bergson, H. (1900). *Le rire: Essai sur la signification du comique*. Félix Alcan.
